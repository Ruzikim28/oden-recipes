Egusi Recipe

Discription

Egusi Soup is a rich, protein-packed Nigerian dish made from ground melon seeds, palm oil, leafy greens, and an assortment of meats and seafood. It features a uniquely savory, nutty flavor profile and a thick texture that makes it one of West Africa's most beloved soups.

Traditionally served during family meals and celebrations, Egusi Soup pairs exceptionally well with swallows like pounded yam, eba, fufu, or amala.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups ground egusi (melon seeds)
  • 1/2 cup red palm oil
  • 1 lb beef, goat meat, or assorted meats (cooked)
  • 1 piece dried fish or stockfish (soaked and cleaned)
  • 1/2 cup ground crayfish
  • 2 Scotch bonnet peppers (habanero), blended
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cups chopped spinach or bitter leaf
  • 3 cups meat stock or water
  • 2 seasoning cubes
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. In a small bowl, mix the ground egusi seeds with a little water to form a thick, smooth paste.
  2. Heat the palm oil in a pot on medium heat for 1–2 minutes (do not bleach). Add the chopped onions and sauté until translucent.
  3. Scoop small lumps of the egusi paste into the pot. Allow it to cook undisturbed for 3–5 minutes so the lumps set.
  4. Gently stir the egusi to break large lumps into smaller pieces, then fry for another 5 minutes, stirring continuously to prevent burning.
  5. Pour in the meat stock, blended peppers, and ground crayfish. Mix thoroughly and bring to a simmer.
  6. Add the pre-cooked meat, stockfish, and seasoning cubes. Cover the pot and simmer for 15 minutes until the oil rises to the top.
  7. Stir in the chopped spinach or leafy greens and adjust salt if needed.
  8. Simmer on low heat for an additional 3–5 minutes before removing from heat. Serve hot with your choice of swallow.
