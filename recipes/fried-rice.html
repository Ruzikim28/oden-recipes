Fried Rice Recipe

Description

Fried Rice is a savory, classic dish made with cooked rice stir-fried in a wok or pan with mixed vegetables, herbs, and aromatics. It features a light, fluffy texture with colorful veggies and chopped green onions sprinkled on top.

Quick and easy to make, this dish works wonderfully as a main meal or as a side paired with chicken, beef, or seafood.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked long-grain rice (preferably cooled or leftover)
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil or sesame oil
  • 1/2 cup finely diced carrots
  • 1/2 cup green peas or chopped green beans
  • 1/2 cup finely chopped spring onions (scallions)
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon ginger, minced
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon white pepper or black pepper
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Heat vegetable oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat.
  2. Add the minced garlic, ginger, and the white parts of the spring onions. Sauté for about 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Add the diced carrots and green peas (or green beans) to the skillet and cook for 2–3 minutes until slightly tender.
  4. Stir in the cooked rice, breaking up any clumps so the grains separate evenly.
  5. Drizzle the soy sauce over the rice and sprinkle with salt and pepper. Stir-fry continuously for 3–5 minutes until the rice is thoroughly heated and evenly coated.
  6. Turn off the heat, garnish generously with the chopped green spring onions, and serve warm.
